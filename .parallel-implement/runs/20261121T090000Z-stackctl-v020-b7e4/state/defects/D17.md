## Finding

`resolve_scalar_join` (`deploy/check/template_check.c:1731-1732`) sets `cursor` on the inner `]` of `!Join [delim, [items]]`, then advances with `cursor+1` and requires `'\0'`. That byte is the outer `]`, so every well-formed short-form `!Join` returns 0. The caller treats the 0 return as unresolved, increments `unresolved_join`, and emits a violation. Legitimate templates that build a User/Role/Group name with short-form `!Join` are falsely rejected by the fail-closed `TEMPLATE_OVERRIDE` gate, and the attempt-2 claim of exact `!Join` resolution is contradicted.
