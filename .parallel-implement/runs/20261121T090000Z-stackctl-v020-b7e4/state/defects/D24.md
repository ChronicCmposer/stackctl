## Finding

The fail-closed path rejects legitimate parameterized NON-CI identities — `RoleName: !Ref SomeParam`, `!GetAtt`, `!ImportValue`, and `!Sub` with a substitution map — even when the resource cannot be the CI identity. Rejecting every unresolvable name regardless of whether it could match the CI identity is an interface policy the CONTRACT does not endorse; only names that could be the CI identity should fail closed.
