## Finding

The intrinsic resolver accepts the two-element `Fn::Sub` sequence form (`[<substitution string>, <substitution map>]`) while ignoring the substitution map. At `deploy/check/template_check.c:960-973` (esp. `:966-969`) only `fn_sub->items[0]` is resolved; any `items[1]` map is discarded. Consumers at `:1448-1453` (operator S3 object scope) and `:1515-1527` (ArtifactReaderPolicy) and the generic path at `:847-851` therefore trust a string whose `${ProjectName}`/`${AWS::AccountId}` placeholders may be rebound by the ignored map. A hostile TEMPLATE_OVERRIDE can pass the gate and rebind ProjectName/AWS::AccountId, enabling cross-project or over-broad S3 grants at deploy time. This is the residual fail-open of D1.
