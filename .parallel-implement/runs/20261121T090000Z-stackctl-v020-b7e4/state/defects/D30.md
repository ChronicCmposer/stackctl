## Finding

Documentation and contract wording still overstate membership/attachment coverage:

- `docs/migration.md:324-330` and CONTRACT `## Amendment 2` say membership/attachment sections "never silently skip" and that their entries must resolve. The entry-level gap in D29 contradicts this for a plain-sequence `UserToGroupAddition.Properties.Users` holding an intrinsic or nested-list entry.
- `docs/migration.md:327-328` calls long-form `Ref` a violating intrinsic-map form. A long-form `Ref` *entry* is in fact accepted; only intrinsic-map *entry* forms (e.g. `Fn::If`, `Fn::ImportValue`) are violations.

Fix direction: state that only intrinsic-map entry forms violate, that a long-form `Ref` entry is accepted, and that the "never silently skips" claim becomes true once D29 is closed.
