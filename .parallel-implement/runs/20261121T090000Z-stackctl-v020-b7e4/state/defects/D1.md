## Finding

The new S3-scope and CI-reader rules silently skip long-form intrinsic `Action`/`Resource` mapping nodes. The helper `scalar_count` at `deploy/check/template_check.c:494-502` is used by the S3-scope and CI-reader checks at `:1286-1324` and `:1348-1384` but handles only scalar/sequence shapes; `{Fn::If: [...]}`, `{Ref: ...}`, `{Fn::Sub: ...}`, and `{Fn::Join: ...}` mapping nodes are neither inspected nor rejected. A template can therefore hide wildcard buckets or CI `s3:PutObject` from the contract-required checks (CONTRACT.md:17,20), and the runtime TEMPLATE_OVERRIDE gate in `deploy/check/BUILD.bazel:15-16` fails open at deploy time.
